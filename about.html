About Reynolds Antique House

Reynolds Antique House presents a curated gallery of antiques, fine art, mirrors, lighting, and decorative objects. This site preserves the original gallery layout and browsing experience while serving a fully local static catalog.

Historical archive material — including stories from the prior gallery lineage such as Jacqueline Kennedy’s 1963 visit — is retained as heritage context. Product descriptions and maker names are preserved as provenance.
