Since 1905

A family house. Still family run.

The story begins in 1896, when Anderson Smith founded The Clover House of Antiques. The firm later passed into a partnership of the Kano Wells family and Brooke Reynolds. In 1905, Brooke Reynolds acquired the outright shares and led the company through 1945, the year we mark as our official beginning.

After the Second World War, Brooke handed leadership to his first son, Phillip Reynolds, and Phillip's two brothers, who ran the company together for forty-eight years. In 1993, Brooke's grandson Thomas Reynolds became CEO and renamed the firm Reynolds Antique House. Thomas remains our CEO today. Every piece we handle now, whether bound for sale, auction, or a film set, moves through our home in Oakland, California.
